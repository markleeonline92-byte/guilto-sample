Our Story

Born from a Sweet Compromise

When our founder's father was diagnosed with diabetes, she refused to let him give up his love for traditional Indian sweets. GUILTO was born from that promise — that health should never mean deprivation.

Every sweet we craft uses time-honored recipes, natural sweeteners like stevia and monk fruit, and the finest organic ingredients. No compromise. Pure bliss.

🌱

100% Natural Sweeteners

We use only stevia, monk fruit, and dates — never artificial substitutes.

👐

Handcrafted in Small Batches

Every piece is made by hand in our artisan kitchen for peak freshness.

🔬

Lab-Tested Quality

Every batch is tested to confirm nutrition claims and zero artificial additives.
